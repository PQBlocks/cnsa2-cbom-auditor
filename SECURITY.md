## Reporting a Vulnerability

We prioritize the security and integrity of post-quantum cryptographic auditing tools. If you identify a vulnerability, security flaw, or incorrect classification algorithm:

1. **GitHub Security Advisory**: Open an advisory draft at [Security Advisories](https://github.com/PQBlocks/cnsa2-cbom-auditor/security/advisories/new).
2. **Author Contact**: Email Govind Dubey directly at `govinddubeycse@gmail.com`.
3. **Public Issues**: For non-sensitive implementation bugs, open a standard issue at [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/PQBlocks/cnsa2-cbom-auditor/issues).

Please provide detailed reproduction steps, sample assets or certificates, and expected vs actual classification verdicts.
